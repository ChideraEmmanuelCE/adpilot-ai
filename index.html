Questions

Good to know.

Does AdPilot pay for advertising spend?

No. Advertising media budgets and platform charges are separate from AdPilot's service fee. Businesses remain responsible for their advertising accounts and media payments.

Is the 20% price a guaranteed quote?

No. It is an illustrative introductory benchmark based on 20% of a comparable traditional agency quote for the same agreed scope. Final pricing depends on requirements and delivery scope.

Can AdPilot launch campaigns automatically today?

This website describes the product direction and proposed capabilities. Live campaign execution requires the relevant advertising-platform integrations, authorized accounts, backend services and credentials to be implemented and configured.

Are the dashboard figures real customer results?

No. All dashboard figures shown here are sample data for demonstration and are not customer results or performance claims.

Which businesses can use AdPilot?

AdPilot is intended for companies and teams that want to organize and improve their advertising operations. Contact us to discuss your specific workflow.
